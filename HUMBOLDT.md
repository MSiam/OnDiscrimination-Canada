
# Humboldt Foundation

I discussed with them my vision and previous research on computer vision, robotics and
embodied AI, Prof. Marcus was quite excited about it. He supported my application to the
Humboldt fellowship, experienced researcher track, which is only around 45K euros a year,
which is half the low annual salary I was already taking! [TabI-HostLetter](https://github.com/MSiam/OnDiscrimination-Canada/tree/main/Tab-I)
